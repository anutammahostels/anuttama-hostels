

## ✅ Refund Reflection Across Dashboard + Cross-Wiring Payroll/Accounting (IMPLEMENTED)

All refund data is now reflected across the dashboard, and payroll is cross-wired into accounting.

### Changes Made

1. **Dashboard Stats** — Added 5th "Refunds" stat card with total refunded amount and count
2. **Billing Page** — Added "Refunds" tab showing full refund history (date, student, invoice, amount, method, reason, status)
3. **Receivables** — Added "Refunds" column, adjusted Net Receivable = Gross - Discounts - Received + Refunds, updated exports
4. **Accounting Fee Collections** — Refunds shown as negative/outflow entries alongside collections with net summary
5. **Accounting P&L** — Refunds deducted from income (Net Fee Income = Collections - Refunds), Staff Salaries from payroll added as expense line
6. **Exports** — All Excel and PDF exports updated to include refunds and payroll data
